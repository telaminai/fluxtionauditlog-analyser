2. THE DOCS AUDIT — 62 OF 67, AND THE FIVE ARE WORTH A LINE
================================================================================
The claim is that site pages carry every `context` key. I checked it mechanically rather than by
reading: extracted every literal `put("…")` from the `context()` method body (bounded by brace depth,
so verb echoes are excluded) and grepped the whole of `docs/site` for each.

    67 literal keys in context() · 5 not mentioned anywhere in docs/site:
        createdAt · dispatchOrder · sourceGraphDeclined · timeOrder · workspaceDir

Three of the five have their CONCEPT documented under a prose name — "dispatch order" appears six
times, "time-order report" three, "workspaceRoot" four — but the KEY is never named. For prose that is
fine; for an agent reading `context` and looking for a handle it is not, which is exactly the gap the
new ONBOARDING convention exists to close. `createdAt` and `sourceGraphDeclined` appear to be absent
outright.

Not blocking, and the audit is a real improvement on where this stood yesterday — 62 of 67 with the
remainder identified is a good place to be. Worth one pass to name the five.

**A false finding I did not report, recorded so nobody re-raises it:** `workspaceRoot` and
`workspaceDir` look like one fact under two names. They are not — `workspaceRoot` is the DECLARATION
(`../..`) and `workspaceDir` is where it RESOLVES on this machine. Two facts, correctly named, and an
agent needs both. I checked the code before writing it up.
