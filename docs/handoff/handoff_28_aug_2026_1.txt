OUTCOME
  Reports can table every D-I7 verb: existing `read`, plus `aggregate`, all three `series` shapes,
  and `coverage`. A table's rows are its verb echo's list, default columns are echo keys, and its
  scalar context is built once so screen and PDF say the same thing. The report model can reissue a
  nested call exactly after save/restart without changing the portable properties-file format.

NON-NEGOTIABLE BOUNDARIES
  · One report table invokes one verb. No joins, sort model, `cellWhen`, or chart/PDF-picture work.
  · `rowWhen` remains a predicate over a row's RECORD. It must never silently mean a formula over a
    bucket or coverage row.
  · A denominator or a truncated result is never hidden: coverage includes covered, uncovered and
    excluded rows in graph order; caps say how many rows were omitted.
  · Keep `ReportStore`'s flat properties format and M38.7 unknown-key preservation. This is a
    compatible value encoding at the store edge, not a config-file rewrite.
  · Pure model/renderer behaviour gets tests. Build and run the jar for the Reports tab, and visually
    inspect generated transcripts/screenshots. Every user-visible slice has its own `[Unreleased]`
    CHANGELOG line, `mvn test`, the public-data sweep, and `mkdocs build --strict` for site changes.

ACCEPTANCE — DO NOT CLAIM WITHOUT EVIDENCE
  1. Demo traced log: `read`, aggregate by dimension, series buckets, and coverage all have rows in
     Reports, PDF, and their existing CSV export path.
  2. A series crossings table navigates to its selected record and honours rowWhen.
  3. aggregate with rowWhen renders un-highlighted and returns a warning explaining why.
  4. A nested filter survives save → restart → reopen and runs under the stored scope; a 1.10.0 report
     stays readable.
  5. No-audit coverage includes every node with covered/uncovered/excluded status and its reason.
  6. Screen and PDF scalar lines are byte-identical for a section.
  7. Empty results render their header plus the verb's own reason.
  8. The regenerated scenario 4 transcript proves aggregate table rows; reports guidance no longer
     calls `read` the only v1 table source.
