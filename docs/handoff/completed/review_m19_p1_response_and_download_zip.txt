bundle. It routes CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, mvnw and mvnw.cmd to data/, despite their ProjectFile paths and
the bundle contract requiring them at root. Existing analyser-bundle tests call generate(spec), before
this second packaging pass, so they cannot detect the relocation.

Required correction:

     zip entries, and asserts root CLAUDE/AGENTS/mvnw/mvnw.cmd and executable lifecycle scripts.
