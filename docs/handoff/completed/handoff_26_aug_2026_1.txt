THE ONE DESIGN RULE — D-L1. The panel is a RENDERING of `context`, not a second model. Nothing in it
may read a MainFrame field. Concretely: a pure `ProjectModel.from(Map contextPayload)` builds the five
sections from the same map the action socket returns, and MainFrame calls
`loadedPanel.render(ProjectModel.from(control.context().payload()))`. A fact the panel needs that
`context` lacks is added to `context` FIRST — that is M37.1, and it improves the agent in the same
slice. Gaps known at brief time: project root; who opened the log (you / action socket); the graph's
path; the processors as a LIST with selected + source-found; the tier of each source root; and the
graph section going missing whenever no log is open (`graphPairing` sat inside the store block).
