    pin's point — machine settings stay out — is restated in the updated test.

    the theme at render time. The first cut hardcoded the paper palette and was unreadable under
    dark — the OWNER caught it in live use; fixed and screenshot-verified in both themes.

    degrade loudly about, unlike a user's marker spec. The asymmetry is argued in the code.

    carry glyphs in the image, table-less, by its frozen-shape contract.
