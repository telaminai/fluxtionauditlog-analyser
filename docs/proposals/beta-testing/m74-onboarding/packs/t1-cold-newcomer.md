# DEMO first look (T1)

Track T1: a newcomer's first question about a DEMO audit log.

1. **What happened in this log?** — I am new to this tool and I have an audit log open. In plain words, what happened in it? Then show me where the first risk breach is and what led up to it.
2. **Which parts stayed silent?** — Which parts of the processor never wrote anything to this log? Does that mean they never ran? Tell me how sure you can be.
